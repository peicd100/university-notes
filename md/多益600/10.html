    50-52

    Questions 50 to 52 refer to the following conversation.

    M: Mary, could you help me out with something, please?
    W: Of course.
    W: What do you need?
    M: I'm meeting with a new horror author tomorrow, and I need to reserve the meeting room on the second floor.
    W: I'm not sure you're going to want to use that room.
    M: Why not?
    M: What's going on?
    W: There's currently a remodel of the second-floor offices going on.
    W: It's pretty noisy down there.
    M: Right, I had forgotten about that.
    M: I guess we could do it in my office then.
    W: I can get all the equipment you need brought in here.
    W: Just let me know what you need.
    M: Thank you.
    M: Oh, I don't have a spare access card for the elevator.
    M: Could you go bring her up here to the 5th floor when she arrives?
    W: Sure, it would be my pleasure.

    Good afternoon, Mike.
    I'm calling to let you know the renovations to the new offices are on track and will be done in two weeks.

    131-134

    Important Notice to All Employees

    Following the recent inspection by the Health and Safety Bureau on April 13th, it has been decided that certain changes to our safety guidelines will be made in order to ensure a safer workplace for all employees.
    It was recommended that clearer signs for emergency exits be placed around the office floors as the current signs can be somewhat difficult to see.
    Additionally, the emergency exit maps will be replaced with newer, easier to understand ones.
    Finally, we will be placing extra fire extinguishers around all floors in case of a fire.
    These measures will create a safer environment for everyone.
    A meeting for all staff will be held on May 3rd to further explain these and some other changes in safety procedures.
    Department heads will provide further explanations regarding these changes.
    These changes will go into effect as of May 1st.

    151-153

    To: *@hershellpublications.com

    From: mark_jones@hershellpublications.com

    Subject: Policy regarding purchases

    Good afternoon, all.
    I hope our move to the new office building went smoothly for you all.
    As it is likely some of you need to replace some of your older office equipment and supplies, such as printers, fax machines, photocopiers, ink cartridges, etc., I would like to take this opportunity to remind you of the policy regarding ordering new equipment or supplies.

    First, the company will only authorize replacing equipment if the cost of repairs exceeds that of replacing the equipment entirely.
    If the equipment can be repaired, the company will cover the costs.
    Should different departments require the same supplies, they are to be ordered as a bulk order.
    If you require new equipment or supplies, please submit your order before the end of this week.
    Standard deliveries take three working days, with bulk orders taking up to seven.
